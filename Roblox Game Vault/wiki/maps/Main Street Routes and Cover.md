# Main Street Routes and Cover

Gameplay read of [[Main Street Block]] for whoever sets up spawns and modes (GAME FLOW). Coordinates in studs, ground top at Y 0, street runs along X.

![[2026-10-16-map-pass1-overhead.jpg]]

## Lanes (three-layer layout from [[Hood Warfare 2 Urban Routes]])
| Lane | Where | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| Main street | X -200 to 160, Z -20 to 20 (road) + sidewalks to Z +/-32 | long sightlines, cover from parked cars, trees, stoops, bus shelter, poles |
| Rear alleys | Z +/-74 to 88, full length | tight, dumpsters and wheelie bins, chain-link on one side, fire escapes above |
| Cut-throughs | north X -14 to -2, south X 52 to 64 | short links between street and alley, 12 wide, one dumpster each |
| Cross street | X 160 to 200 | east end, crosswalk, corner store and laundromat corners |
| East alley gap | X 212 to 246, Z -10 to 2 | flank behind the east block |

## Landmarks (for callouts)
- **Corner store** (NE, X 104 to 148, red awning)
- **Laundromat** (SE, X 108 to 148, blue awning)
- **Apartment block** (closes the west end, X -236 to -200, fire escape)
- **Vacant lot** (NW, X -200 to -160, fenced) and **parking lot** (SW)
- **Bus stop** (north sidewalk, X ~30)
- Graffiti walls in both cut-throughs (SPAT, BRAKE...), good for callouts too.

## Cover inventory
- 10 parked cars on the street, 2 in the west lot (block movement and bullets once guns raycast against them).
- 16 tree trunks (thin, leaves don't block).
- Stoops with rails on every rowhouse, iron front fences (see-through).
- 9 dumpsters, 24 wheelie bins in the alleys.
- Utility poles and streetlight poles (thin).

## Suggestions for GAME FLOW (not built, not decided)
- TURF WAR zones from the design doc: corner store, vacant lot (as the "court"), laundromat. All three already exist as landmarks.
- Spawns: one team behind the apartment block / west lots, the other behind the east block. Both have alley and street exits so spawns aren't one straight sightline.
- The main street is long (360 studs). Consider a blocker (truck, construction fence) halfway to cut the longest sniper line, or leave it as the sniper lane.
- Rooftops aren't reachable yet. Fire escapes on alley side could become climb routes later.
