# Art Direction

Hub for the look of O Blah Warfare. Built from the first 5 references (2026-10-16).

## The look in one line
A gritty, made-up city at night or under grey skies, with realistic guns that pop through color cosmetics.

## World (shared, map is GAME FLOW)
- Dark or overcast, desaturated, hazy distance. See [[Overcast Rowhouse Street]].
- Few warm lights and hard shadows at night. See [[Night Porch Firefight]].
- Brick rowhouses, fences, power lines, parked cars. Lots of cover.
- Fictional place only. No real streets, signs, or landmarks.
- Big mature street trees whose canopy closes over the street. Real branching trunks, never one straight cylinder.
- Parked cars are real-looking old beaters, not too many. No blobby AI cars.
- Graffiti must look like real spray paint, layered, with hood-sounding tags. See [[Graffiti Style]].
- Built so far: [[Main Street Block]].

## Guns (COMBAT)
- Realistic shapes, matte black, worn edges. See [[Weapon Lineup]].
- Swappable attachments that change the silhouette.
- Cosmetics: one bright accent color per gun ([[Color Grip Pistols Key Art]]) and full themed skins ([[Pink Sniper Skin]]).
- Muzzle flashes light up the dark scene.

## Hard lines
- Cosmetic only, no pay-to-win, no paid random loot boxes.
- No gang names, hand signs, real locations, gore, or blood.
- No store weapons. Our own meshes or generated ones.
