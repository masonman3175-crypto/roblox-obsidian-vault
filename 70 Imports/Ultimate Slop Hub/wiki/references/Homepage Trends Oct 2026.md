## What it is
Two screenshots of Cade's Roblox "Recommended For You" grid, 2026-10-17. About 60 games, ratings mostly 84-98%.

## Why we care
Shows which titles and thumbnails win right now, so our games look like they belong on the homepage.

## Breakdown
- **"+1 X" titles everywhere:** +1 Speed Keyboard Escape, +1 Strength Per Click, +1 Mine Per Click, +1 Stone Skipping, +1 Wings Obby Escape, +1 Jump Slime Climb, +1 Money Roll, +1 Assassin Leveling, +1 Monster Evolution, +1 Fart to Break Doors.
- **Other hot words:** Steal (Steal a Verity, Steal an Egg, Steal ASMR), ASMR (butter, keycaps, squishies), Escape, Eggs, Brainrots, Keyboard.
- **Title tags:** `[x2]`, `[UPD]`, `[NEW!]`, `[PETS]`, `[TRADING]`, emoji in brackets. Signals an event or update.
- **Thumbnail formula:**
  - Classic noob or bacon-hair avatar, big shocked or smug face.
  - **Weak to strong** in one image: skeleton hand > hand > buff hand with +1 / +67 / +999 popups; 67 > 999,999 with an arrow; 1W > 1B W; Day 1 > Day 100.
  - Floating "+1" text, rainbow trails, giant numbers ($964,642.5B, 100M+, +999T).
  - **Stud-height walls:** "+1 Jump Slime Climb" shows a wall labeled 3, 7, 8, 9, 15, 25 studs; another thumbnail has stairs labeled 10 / 50 / 100 / 999 / 99999 Studs. Same idea as our tower.
  - Saturated colors, sky blue, lime grass, chunky plastic look.

## How we'd build it in Roblox
- **Title:** "+1 [Stat] [Verb] [Place]", e.g. "+1 Strength Tower Climb". `[UPD]` tags only for real updates.
- **Thumbnail:** stage it in the place: noob avatar, before/after split, big +1 popups (BillboardGui text), stud wall with height labels behind. `screen_capture`, finish in an image editor.
- **Compliance check:** no other games' IP, licensed characters, or real brands in thumbnails. See [[70 Imports/Ultimate Slop Hub/blueprint/Monetization And Compliance|Monetization And Compliance]].
- **Steps:**
  1. Pick stat + verb + setting.
  2. Build the thumbnail scene in the place itself.
  3. Make 2-3 thumbnails and A/B test them (Roblox thumbnail testing, details unverified).
