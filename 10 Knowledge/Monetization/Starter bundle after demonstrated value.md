# Starter bundle after demonstrated value

> [!note] Evidence boundary
> This is an original implementation/design proposal. Sources below supply background or API guidance; proposed timings, quantities, and business results are not established facts.

## Decision
A starter bundle should explain an optional shortcut or cosmetic identity, not rescue a deliberately miserable first minute.

## Implementation and rationale
Offer it through the shop after the player understands the first loop. Show each included item and whether currency is consumed or permanent. Do not use a resetting timer or a permanent fake discount. Provide a normal free upgrade path in the same progression system. Track fulfilment and satisfaction, not only clicks.

## Acceptance test
- [ ] A completely unpaid new account completes onboarding and the first zone. Cancelling or dismissing the bundle never prevents continuation or immediately reopens the offer.
