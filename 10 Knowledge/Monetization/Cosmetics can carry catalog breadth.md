# Cosmetics can carry catalog breadth

> [!note] Evidence boundary
> This is an original implementation/design proposal. Sources below supply background or API guidance; proposed timings, quantities, and business results are not established facts.

## Decision
Offer distinct visual families instead of selling the same mandatory power upgrade many times.

## Implementation and rationale
Draft families: candy wrappers, neon delivery trails, toy-cart carry styles, base floor themes, display pedestals, nameplates, celebration emotes, and guardian photo props. Preview the exact appearance on the player's own scene. Keep gameplay hitboxes and other players' readability consistent across cosmetic variants.

## Acceptance test
- [ ] Swap every skin on the same item and verify identical functional stats and collision. Other players can reduce visual effects without losing gameplay information.
