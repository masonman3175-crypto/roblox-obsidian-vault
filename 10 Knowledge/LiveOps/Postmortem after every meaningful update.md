# Postmortem after every meaningful update

> [!note] Evidence boundary
> This is an original implementation/design proposal. Sources below supply background or API guidance; proposed timings, quantities, and business results are not established facts.

## Decision
Record what changed, what happened, and what to do next without rewriting the original prediction.

## Implementation and rationale
Compare retention, early funnel completion, economy flow, errors, and qualitative feedback with the pre-update period. Explain changes in acquisition mix and observation windows. Note what the event cost to produce, not just player engagement. An inconclusive test is still useful research when assumptions and limits are documented.
