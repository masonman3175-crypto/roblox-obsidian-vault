# A sustainable release calendar

> [!note] Evidence boundary
> This is an original implementation/design proposal. Sources below supply background or API guidance; proposed timings, quantities, and business results are not established facts.

## Decision
Plan small content additions around reusable systems and leave capacity for fixes.

## Implementation and rationale
Draft a four-step cadence: polish and telemetry, a small cosmetic/content family, a bounded gameplay event, then a maintenance and evaluation window. This is a capacity plan, not a commitment to weekly releases. Track art, scripting, testing, localization, and moderation dependencies separately. Do not sell a recurring stream the team cannot produce.

## Acceptance test
- [ ] Estimate each release from actual asset throughput. If a task needs a new service or transaction system, classify it as an expansion rather than a routine content drop.
