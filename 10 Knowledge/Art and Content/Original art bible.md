# Original art bible

> [!note] Evidence boundary
> This is an original implementation/design proposal. Sources below supply background or API guidance; proposed timings, quantities, and business results are not established facts.

## Decision
Make a short visual contract before producing a hundred assets.

## Implementation and rationale
Define chunky toy proportions, large readable eyes, simple materials, one accent per species, and a limited palette per zone. Maintain front and side reference views and a standard scale marker. Record which aspects of the user's screenshot are mood references and which shapes are newly designed. Do not recreate its exact creature or map.

## Acceptance test
- [ ] Put ten assets side by side. They share lighting and scale while remaining distinguishable at gameplay distance; no design depends on an existing game's distinctive character.
