# Inspiration without a reskin

> [!note] Evidence boundary
> This is an original implementation/design proposal. Sources below supply background or API guidance; proposed timings, quantities, and business results are not established facts.

## Decision
Keep the genre's readability while replacing its distinctive world and at least one core interaction.

## Implementation and rationale
Borrow broad lessons such as visible goals, bright contrast, collectible spectacle, and short runs. Change the fiction, collectible silhouettes, route structure, risk rules, upgrade purpose, and social payoff. Document the difference in the concept pitch. Do not trace the supplied screenshot's guardian or recreate another game's exact interface art.
