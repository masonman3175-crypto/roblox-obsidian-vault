# Shareable moments without fake spectacle

> [!note] Evidence boundary
> This is an original implementation/design proposal. Sources below supply background or API guidance; proposed timings, quantities, and business results are not established facts.

## Decision
Make the real gameplay produce clear captures that can also support truthful marketing.

## Implementation and rationale
Provide optional camera framing at a safe display area, original cosmetic props, and a small celebration pose. Do not block active routes or use paid visual effects to hide opponents. Keep photography optional and allow ordinary players to understand the game without external sharing. Use private sessions for recording when appropriate.

## Acceptance test
- [ ] Capture a ten-second original run and a base showcase. The advertised object and action exist in normal play, and camera mode cleanly returns input control.
