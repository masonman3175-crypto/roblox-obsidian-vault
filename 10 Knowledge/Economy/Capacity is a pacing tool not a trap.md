# Capacity is a pacing tool not a trap

> [!note] Evidence boundary
> This is an original implementation/design proposal. Sources below supply background or API guidance; proposed timings, quantities, and business results are not established facts.

## Decision
Free capacity must support the normal loop, and a full inventory must have useful choices.

## Implementation and rationale
Offer free management options: sell an unwanted duplicate, move a display, or store a pending reward. Paid display slots can be optional convenience only if free players can still finish the intended session goals. Separate inventory ownership capacity from the visual number of displays in the world.

## Acceptance test
- [ ] Grant a purchased cosmetic or legitimate quest reward at full capacity. The entitlement persists, a free management route exists, and no purchase prompt appears automatically.
