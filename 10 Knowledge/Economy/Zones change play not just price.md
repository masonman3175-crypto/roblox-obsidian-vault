# Zones change play not just price

> [!note] Evidence boundary
> This is an original implementation/design proposal. Sources below supply background or API guidance; proposed timings, quantities, and business results are not established facts.

## Decision
Each zone should add one readable mechanic that existing systems can express.

## Implementation and rationale
Example sequence: simple crossing, moving conveyor, guardian patrol window, cooperative switch, and optional risky shortcut. Keep rare collectible spectacle visible before the unlock without blocking the beginner route. Avoid creating ten distinct services for ten cosmetic biomes. Define a content budget per zone.

## Acceptance test
- [ ] A tester describes the difference between two adjacent zones without mentioning numbers or colors. The zone works alone and in a busy server.
