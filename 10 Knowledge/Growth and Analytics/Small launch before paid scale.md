# Small launch before paid scale

> [!note] Evidence boundary
> This is an original implementation/design proposal. Sources below supply background or API guidance; proposed timings, quantities, and business results are not established facts.

## Decision
Test load, loop clarity, return behavior, and purchases before spending heavily on traffic.

## Implementation and rationale
Start with a controlled group and no live paid products, then test a published environment and a small approved acquisition experiment if desired. Fix obvious load or tutorial failures before increasing reach. Keep a written budget and stop condition. No ad campaign is created or purchased as part of this research pack.

## Acceptance test
- [ ] The release checklist is complete, new testers understand the loop, and receipt/save recovery tests pass. The acquisition plan includes a maximum spend and an evaluation window.
