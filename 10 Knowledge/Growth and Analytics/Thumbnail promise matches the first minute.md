# Thumbnail promise matches the first minute

> [!note] Evidence boundary
> This is an original implementation/design proposal. Sources below supply background or API guidance; proposed timings, quantities, and business results are not established facts.

## Decision
Use a dramatic truthful moment that the player can actually experience.

## Implementation and rationale
Draft one composition showing a giant snack guardian and a visibly carried prize; another showing a cooperative escape. Use original models and restrained text. Test legibility at small size before producing many variants. Do not advertise an absent boss, fake rarity odds, free Robux, or a gameplay mechanic that exists only in the image.

## Acceptance test
- [ ] A tester enters from the thumbnail and finds its promised action in the opening session. Record expectation mismatch separately from whether the picture gets clicks.
