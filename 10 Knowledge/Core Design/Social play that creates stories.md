# Social play that creates stories

> [!note] Evidence boundary
> This is an original implementation/design proposal. Sources below supply background or API guidance; proposed timings, quantities, and business results are not established facts.

## Decision
Make cooperation and rivalry produce observable moments without requiring chat to explain them.

## Implementation and rationale
Try a two-person carry bonus, a rescue gadget, or a public delivery celebration. Show who contributed and give each player a reward once. Keep mutual activity useful even when players have unequal progression. Use original emotes and expressive objects instead of relying on borrowed meme characters.

## Acceptance test
- [ ] Two strangers can collaborate using only visible prompts. A player declining participation can continue solo and is not repeatedly prompted.
