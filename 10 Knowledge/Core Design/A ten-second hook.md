# A ten-second hook

> [!note] Evidence boundary
> This is an original implementation/design proposal. Sources below supply background or API guidance; proposed timings, quantities, and business results are not established facts.

## Decision
Show the toy, the risk, and the destination in the spawn camera rather than explaining the entire game in text.

## Implementation and rationale
Place an animated original snack guardian across a short crossing from the player's empty display. A large carried snack silhouette makes the action legible to spectators. The first object is easy to obtain; a visible harder target promises future mastery. Avoid making the title's promise depend on a late unlock.

## Acceptance test
- [ ] Show an uncaptioned ten-second capture; testers should identify the action and the payoff. Treat the target duration as a project hypothesis, not a proven platform threshold.
