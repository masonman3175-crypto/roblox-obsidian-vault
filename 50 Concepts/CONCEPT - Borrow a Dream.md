# CONCEPT - Borrow a Dream

**Status:** original concept proposal; not a validated market opportunity.

## Hook
Collect dream-toys from a changing pillow maze and wake them in a display room.

## Distinctive mechanic
A shared map rotates safe doors; the player keeps discovered toys after a failed current run.

## Art and optional products
Pillow whales and moon bunnies; bedroom themes and soft particle trails. No catalog price or revenue prediction is established. Keep the core loop free and use the shared fulfilment architecture.

## Smallest prototype
One room, two door states, and protected banked ownership.

## Evaluation
Test whether a new player understands the first goal, wants another attempt after failure, and can describe the difference from the reference games. Reject or revise the concept before creating dozens of assets if those observations are weak.
