# CONCEPT - Rescue a Rubber Duck

**Status:** original concept proposal; not a validated market opportunity.

## Hook
Recover original toy ducks from a flooded workshop.

## Distinctive mechanic
Cooperative floodgates create optional shortcuts while solo routes remain available.

## Art and optional products
Wind-up ducks and bathtub robots; bathhouse decor and ripple trails. No catalog price or revenue prediction is established. Keep the core loop free and use the shared fulfilment architecture.

## Smallest prototype
One gate, one solo route, and equal-credit cooperative delivery.

## Evaluation
Test whether a new player understands the first goal, wants another attempt after failure, and can describe the difference from the reference games. Reject or revise the concept before creating dozens of assets if those observations are weak.

## Supporting decisions
- [[Co-op goals with equitable credit]]
- [[Fun at one concurrent player]]
- [[Inspiration without a reskin]]
- [[Greybox before content volume]]
