# CONCEPT - Snatch a Snack

**Status:** original concept proposal; not a validated market opportunity.

## Hook
Carry absurd snack-creatures across changing delivery routes to a toy-box base.

## Distinctive mechanic
A conveyor crossing changes direction, so players choose timing and route rather than buying endless speed.

## Art and optional products
Soda slugs, noodle kites, waffle crabs; display themes, wrapper skins, delivery trails. No catalog price or revenue prediction is established. Keep the core loop free and use the shared fulfilment architecture.

## Smallest prototype
A single guardian, two routes, three snack families, and one display upgrade.

## Evaluation
Test whether a new player understands the first goal, wants another attempt after failure, and can describe the difference from the reference games. Reject or revise the concept before creating dozens of assets if those observations are weak.
