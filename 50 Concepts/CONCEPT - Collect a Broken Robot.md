# CONCEPT - Collect a Broken Robot

**Status:** original concept proposal; not a validated market opportunity.

## Hook
Salvage expressive toy robot parts from a junkyard and assemble fixed recipes.

## Distinctive mechanic
Deterministic assembly gives duplicates a purpose without selling random upgrade success.

## Art and optional products
Toaster walkers and floppy antenna bots; workbench themes and spark colors. No catalog price or revenue prediction is established. Keep the core loop free and use the shared fulfilment architecture.

## Smallest prototype
Three parts, one recipe, and inventory locks during assembly.

## Evaluation
Test whether a new player understands the first goal, wants another attempt after failure, and can describe the difference from the reference games. Reject or revise the concept before creating dozens of assets if those observations are weak.
