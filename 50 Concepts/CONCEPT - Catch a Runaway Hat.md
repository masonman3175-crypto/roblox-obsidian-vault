# CONCEPT - Catch a Runaway Hat

**Status:** original concept proposal; not a validated market opportunity.

## Hook
Capture hats with tiny feet and deliver them to a fashion parade.

## Distinctive mechanic
Hats have learnable movement quirks; players improve interception instead of damage numbers.

## Art and optional products
Umbrella top hats and noodle caps; parade floats and outfit poses. No catalog price or revenue prediction is established. Keep the core loop free and use the shared fulfilment architecture.

## Smallest prototype
Two movement patterns on one short street, with controller prompts.

## Evaluation
Test whether a new player understands the first goal, wants another attempt after failure, and can describe the difference from the reference games. Reject or revise the concept before creating dozens of assets if those observations are weak.

## Supporting decisions
- [[Guardian behavior that players can learn]]
- [[Controller focus is a first-class state]]
- [[Inspiration without a reskin]]
- [[Greybox before content volume]]
