# CONCEPT - Save the Last Dumpling

**Status:** original concept proposal; not a validated market opportunity.

## Hook
Rescue dumpling creatures from a moving buffet guarded by kitchen gadgets.

## Distinctive mechanic
Players choose ingredient families to complete recipes through deterministic set collection.

## Art and optional products
Dumpling comets, bao balloons, noodle knots; serving trays and kitchen decor. No catalog price or revenue prediction is established. Keep the core loop free and use the shared fulfilment architecture.

## Smallest prototype
One buffet loop, a readable guardian, and one guaranteed recipe.

## Evaluation
Test whether a new player understands the first goal, wants another attempt after failure, and can describe the difference from the reference games. Reject or revise the concept before creating dozens of assets if those observations are weak.
