# CONCEPT - Rescue a Sock

**Status:** original concept proposal; not a validated market opportunity.

## Hook
Recover mismatched living socks from a gigantic laundry conveyor.

## Distinctive mechanic
A pair-matching collection objective changes which item is valuable to each player.

## Art and optional products
Striped sock worms and detergent blobs; laundry-room themes and basket skins. No catalog price or revenue prediction is established. Keep the core loop free and use the shared fulfilment architecture.

## Smallest prototype
Two pairs, one conveyor hazard, and a safe return basket.

## Evaluation
Test whether a new player understands the first goal, wants another attempt after failure, and can describe the difference from the reference games. Reject or revise the concept before creating dozens of assets if those observations are weak.
