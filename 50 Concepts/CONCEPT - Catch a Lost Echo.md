# CONCEPT - Catch a Lost Echo

**Status:** original concept proposal; not a validated market opportunity.

## Hook
Collect visible sound-creatures and arrange them into a silent-compatible musical display.

## Distinctive mechanic
The collection creates short loop patterns, while all critical play also has visual cues.

## Art and optional products
Bell blobs and drum frogs; instrument palettes, stage decor, and celebratory poses. No catalog price or revenue prediction is established. Keep the core loop free and use the shared fulfilment architecture.

## Smallest prototype
Three sound-creatures, a visual rhythm gate, and independent mix controls.

## Evaluation
Test whether a new player understands the first goal, wants another attempt after failure, and can describe the difference from the reference games. Reject or revise the concept before creating dozens of assets if those observations are weak.
