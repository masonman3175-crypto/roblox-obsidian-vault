# CONCEPT - Carry a Castle

**Status:** original concept proposal; not a validated market opportunity.

## Hook
Haul miniature living castles to a tabletop kingdom.

## Distinctive mechanic
Temporary route-building choices create the escape, rather than a permanent speed treadmill.

## Art and optional products
Teapot keeps and biscuit towers; tabletop mats and miniature flags. No catalog price or revenue prediction is established. Keep the core loop free and use the shared fulfilment architecture.

## Smallest prototype
One bridge choice, one castle family, and three display plots.

## Evaluation
Test whether a new player understands the first goal, wants another attempt after failure, and can describe the difference from the reference games. Reject or revise the concept before creating dozens of assets if those observations are weak.
