# CONCEPT - Return a Stolen Color

**Status:** original concept proposal; not a validated market opportunity.

## Hook
Carry color-creatures through a grayscale toy world and restore your base palette.

## Distinctive mechanic
Delivered families reveal local decorative changes; competitive stats remain independent of paid palettes.

## Art and optional products
Paint slugs and prism crabs; cosmetic palette packs and brush trails. No catalog price or revenue prediction is established. Keep the core loop free and use the shared fulfilment architecture.

## Smallest prototype
Three color families, equivalent icon labels, and one visual world change.

## Evaluation
Test whether a new player understands the first goal, wants another attempt after failure, and can describe the difference from the reference games. Reject or revise the concept before creating dozens of assets if those observations are weak.

- [[Bright style without unreadable information]]
- [[Inspiration without a reskin]]
