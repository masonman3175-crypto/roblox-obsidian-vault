# CONCEPT - Borrow a Cloud

**Status:** original concept proposal; not a validated market opportunity.

## Hook
Catch tiny weather-creatures and deliver them to a rooftop weather museum.

## Distinctive mechanic
Clouds change route conditions; players combine temporary weather effects with a predictable escape route.

## Art and optional products
Puffy square clouds, rainbow snails, umbrella crabs; skybox themes and display pedestals. No catalog price or revenue prediction is established. Keep the core loop free and use the shared fulfilment architecture.

## Smallest prototype
One rooftop circuit with rain and wind changing a single route.

## Evaluation
Test whether a new player understands the first goal, wants another attempt after failure, and can describe the difference from the reference games. Reject or revise the concept before creating dozens of assets if those observations are weak.

## Supporting decisions
- [[Guardian behavior that players can learn]]
- [[Zones change play not just price]]
- [[Inspiration without a reskin]]
- [[Greybox before content volume]]
