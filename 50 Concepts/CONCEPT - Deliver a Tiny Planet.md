# CONCEPT - Deliver a Tiny Planet

**Status:** original concept proposal; not a validated market opportunity.

## Hook
Move wobbling miniature planets to a personal space shelf.

## Distinctive mechanic
The carry route uses rotating safe windows, not a copied base-stealing map.

## Art and optional products
Marble planets with toy rings; star trails, shelf themes, and astronaut emotes. No catalog price or revenue prediction is established. Keep the core loop free and use the shared fulfilment architecture.

## Smallest prototype
One orbit obstacle and three visibly different planets.

## Evaluation
Test whether a new player understands the first goal, wants another attempt after failure, and can describe the difference from the reference games. Reject or revise the concept before creating dozens of assets if those observations are weak.
