# 25 additional sound-design references

These are **original concept references**, not downloaded artwork, licensed files, or tested Roblox implementations. Each entry provides a distinct design brief for AI-assisted development. Validate accessibility, performance, and implementation in Studio before shipping.

Creator Hub background: https://create.roblox.com/docs/sound/groups

1. **Scoreboard tick** — Short dry tick with restrained volume on score change.
2. **Basket swish** — Soft net-layer sound, distinct from rim contact.
3. **Perfect release tone** — Brief clear confirmation separate from error sounds.
4. **Match countdown** — Three short cues and one different start cue.
5. **Ready state toggle** — Two related pitches for ready and unready.
6. **Invite received** — Nonintrusive cue with adjustable notification volume.
7. **Party joined** — Single gentle harmonic cue, not a long fanfare.
8. **Reward reveal** — Short ascending phrase on revealed reward.
9. **Level up accent** — Two to three note positive stinger.
10. **Quest complete chime** — Quick unique chime, avoid triggering multiple overlaps.
11. **Checkpoint activated** — Quiet confirmation distinct from victory music.
12. **Pickup item sound** — Fast pickup tap with pitch variation and cap.
13. **Collectible rarity reveal** — Use one shared base effect plus rarity layer.
14. **Menu slider tick** — Subtle tick with throttling when slider moves.
15. **Page transition** — Optional short whoosh that can be muted.
16. **Failed action feedback** — Gentle neutral low note, never harshly loud.
17. **Search no results** — Minimal two-note cue accompanying readable text.
18. **Lobby ambience** — Soft loop with clean fade in and fade out.
19. **Indoor ambience** — Low environmental bed with controlled reflections.
20. **Outdoor nature bed** — Gentle layers with rare spatial accents.
21. **Vehicle acceleration** — Pitch follows motion smoothly with bounded range.
22. **Door open close** — Paired cues matching motion and distance.
23. **Footstep surface set** — Distinct quiet material groups using approved audio.
24. **Water splash** — Layer transient with softer tail for depth.
25. **UI accessibility cues** — Offer visual equivalents and independent mute controls.
