# Animation reference links

Links only: nothing here is downloaded or mirrored, and content belongs to its owners. Check licences before reusing any footage or assets. Search links open live results, so what they show changes and has not been vetted. Pair these with [STYLE-BRIEFS.md](STYLE-BRIEFS.md).

## Official Roblox
- [Animation overview](https://create.roblox.com/docs/animation) - rigs, Animation Editor, playback.
- [Animation Editor](https://create.roblox.com/docs/animation/editor) - keyframes, curves, priorities.
- [Animation events](https://create.roblox.com/docs/animation/events) - markers for hit frames and sounds.
- [creator-docs source (GitHub)](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/animation/index.md) - CC BY 4.0, same as `official-docs/`.

## Tools
- [Mixamo](https://www.mixamo.com/) - free rigged mocap library; needs retargeting to R15 and a licence check.
- [Mixamo plugin thread (DevForum)](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/mixamo-plugin-released/2298825) - community plugin announcement; early release.
- Moon Animator - popular community timeline plugin; search the Creator Store for the current listing and check the publisher before installing.

## Principles
- [Twelve basic principles of animation (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve_basic_principles_of_animation) - squash and stretch, anticipation, follow-through, timing.
- *The Illusion of Life* (Thomas and Johnston, 1981) and *The Animator's Survival Kit* (Richard Williams) - standard books.
