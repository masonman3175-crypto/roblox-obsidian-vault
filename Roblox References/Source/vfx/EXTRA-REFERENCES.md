# 25 additional visual feedback references

These are **original concept references**, not downloaded artwork, licensed files, or tested Roblox implementations. Each entry provides a distinct design brief for AI-assisted development. Validate accessibility, performance, and implementation in Studio before shipping.

Creator Hub background: https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/ParticleEmitter

1. **Basket score burst** — Small particles near the hoop, short lifetime and limited emission.
2. **Perfect timing spark** — Tiny stroke and glow accent synchronized with timing feedback.
3. **Court floor reflection** — Subtle material response without full-screen bloom.
4. **Team capture pulse** — Ring expands from capture point and fades rapidly.
5. **Checkpoint shimmer** — Small vertical particle ribbon when objective is activated.
6. **Quest complete confetti** — Brief local confetti emission; disable during reduced effects.
7. **Collectible outline** — Slow opacity pulse on nearby collectible models.
8. **Power-up duration halo** — Ring uses texture and timer so color is not sole cue.
9. **Portal edge glow** — Edge particles suggest motion while center remains legible.
10. **Rain splash** — Short bursts near surfaces with sensible performance limits.
11. **Snow footprint** — Small ground decal or temporary texture marks with expiry.
12. **Day-night atmospheric tint** — Smooth Lighting transitions avoiding sudden flashes.
13. **Water entry ripple** — Expanding textured ring at water contact.
14. **Wind gust leaves** — Small directional particles with capped count.
15. **Vehicle boost trail** — Attach low-count trail, stop it cleanly on end.
16. **Finish line streamer** — Short ribbons triggered only on finished state.
17. **Build placement ghost** — Translucent preview and clear valid/invalid label.
18. **Menu selection halo** — Soft stroke-based halo rather than excessive glow.
19. **Inventory rarity border** — Consistent border motif plus readable text rarity.
20. **Rank-up star burst** — Localized sparkle at the rank card, not full screen.
21. **Level completion wave** — Lightweight expanding ring on the final objective.
22. **Camera focus vignette** — Soft reversible edge fade used sparingly.
23. **Healer support sparkle** — Friendly non-graphic visual feedback with low particle budget.
24. **Soccer goal net burst** — Brief mesh or sprite burst near net region.
25. **Environmental dust mote** — Sparse ambient particles that scale down on low graphics.
