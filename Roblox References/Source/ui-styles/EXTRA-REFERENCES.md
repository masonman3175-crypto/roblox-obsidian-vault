# 40 additional UI composition references

These are **original concept references**, not downloaded artwork, licensed files, or tested Roblox implementations. Each entry provides a distinct design brief for AI-assisted development. Validate accessibility, performance, and implementation in Studio before shipping.

Creator Hub background: https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/UIGridLayout

1. **Adaptive sidebar** — Use a fixed-width sidebar plus flexible content frame; collapse navigation labels at narrow widths.
2. **Quest tracker** — Stack compact objective rows with progress indicators and a minimized state.
3. **Equipment comparison** — Two balanced columns for equipped versus highlighted items; show differences semantically.
4. **Team selection** — Large team cards with clear availability and selection state.
5. **Party lobby** — Player slots, ready indicators, invite controls and a visible start requirement.
6. **Basketball scoreboard** — Quarter, shot clock, team score, possession and foul indicators in a compact HUD.
7. **Shot meter** — Readable timing window, accessibility fallback, consistent result feedback.
8. **Match recap** — Summary, personal stats, progression and next-step button in one hierarchy.
9. **Spectator panel** — Player switcher, camera mode and hideable controls.
10. **Practice drill menu** — Difficulty, short objectives, best result and repeat controls.
11. **Mobile thumb zones** — Keep frequent actions within comfortable reach while preserving center visibility.
12. **Controller focus map** — Ensure tab order, focused highlights and predictable focus return.
13. **Trade review** — Clearly separate offers, confirmations and final acceptance states.
14. **Collection album** — Filtered grids with unlocked and unknown states.
15. **Crafting recipe** — Ingredients, owned quantities, requirements and preview in one panel.
16. **Server browser** — Region, size, rules and join state in scan-friendly rows.
17. **Pet care dashboard** — Needs, tasks, activities and progress organized by urgency.
18. **Tycoon purchase board** — Price, prerequisites and next unlock clearly distinguished.
19. **Vehicle garage** — Rotation preview, key stats and customization controls.
20. **Character creator** — Category tabs, live preview, undo and reset actions.
21. **Achievement shelf** — Rarity, progress and earned date with helpful sorting.
22. **Daily checklist** — Short repeatable objectives without intrusive overlays.
23. **Map legend** — Distinct shapes and labels beyond color alone.
24. **Settings preview** — Instant visual feedback with reversible changes.
25. **Inventory bulk select** — Clear selection counts and undo before destructive operations.
26. **Minimal loading interface** — Compact progress label, tips and no fake percentage.
27. **Ranked queue state** — Visible cancellation, team size and matchmaking status.
28. **Skill tree branches** — Progressive disclosure with prerequisites and zoom controls.
29. **Season progression** — Track tiers and unlocked rewards without overwhelming tiny screens.
30. **Photo mode controls** — Hideable overlays, camera hints and safe save feedback.
31. **Text chat accessibility** — Readable contrast and resizing considerations.
32. **Tutorial spotlight** — Highlight one actionable control at a time and allow replay.
33. **Leaderboard compact** — Sticky heading and readable rank changes.
34. **Round voting** — Options with clear timer and one vote state.
35. **Creator attribution panel** — Source, creator and license fields alongside preview.
36. **Searchable visual gallery** — Filter by tags, source and layout and show attribution.
37. **Confirmation dialog** — State the consequence and keep cancel easy to see.
38. **Responsive shop grid** — Reflow cards without shrinking text to illegibility.
39. **Profile header** — Avatar, role, progression and relevant actions.
40. **Notification center** — Group updates, prevent duplicate stacking and support dismissal.
