# 20 additional cinematic references

These are **original concept references**, not downloaded artwork, licensed files, or tested Roblox implementations. Each entry provides a distinct design brief for AI-assisted development. Validate accessibility, performance, and implementation in Studio before shipping.

Creator Hub background: https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Camera

1. **Arena establishing shot** — Move from signage to a wide court view before returning control.
2. **Player introduction** — Frame avatar nameplate then widen to show teammates.
3. **Team lineup reveal** — Pan across players with readable spacing and short duration.
4. **Tipoff anticipation** — Hold court center briefly while gameplay controls remain disabled.
5. **Victory freeze-frame** — Hold clean match result framing then open recap.
6. **Replay angle swap** — Switch from wide replay to side angle at a planned beat.
7. **Replay slow moment** — Apply deliberate playback pacing only to the replay sequence.
8. **Training coach intro** — Show the drill area and objective before releasing control.
9. **Location title reveal** — Overlay short location text over a stable environmental shot.
10. **Dialogue two-shot** — Keep both actors in frame to establish spatial context.
11. **Dialogue reaction insert** — Cut to receiver and return without disorienting the viewer.
12. **Shop item showcase** — Orbit a display stand without losing background context.
13. **Quest handoff** — Move attention from NPC to quest destination.
14. **Travel arrival** — Use a short landmark reveal and skip option.
15. **Round opening flythrough** — Show key landmarks in an arena without hidden competitive information.
16. **Celebration camera** — Widen for the team emote then transition back to standard view.
17. **Photo opportunity** — Hold a composed angle and restore the prior camera afterward.
18. **Cinematic tutorial** — Show the target object with labels and player control restoration.
19. **Event stage reveal** — Move from audience overview to center-stage focal point.
20. **Accessibility skip route** — Skip every scene via the same action and always restore controls.
