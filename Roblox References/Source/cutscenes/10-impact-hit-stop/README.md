# 10. Impact Hit-Stop

Brief freeze/slow on heavy hits plus a white flash frame.

**Roblox recipe (from general Roblox knowledge, not tested in Studio):** AdjustSpeed 0.1 on tracks for 0.08s, overlay flash.
